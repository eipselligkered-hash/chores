# Weekend workload proposal (review item 1)

**Status: proposal only. Nothing has been changed in the live data.**

## Goal
Tasks that count toward the 300-minute weekend cap currently need about **403 min/week** in summer (385 in winter, when the Apr–Sep tasks pause). That is more than the cap, so Carry over grows every week. With the changes below, the load drops to **about 279 min/week in summer and 267 in winter**. That leaves headroom for one-offs, and Carry over drains.

Not counted toward the cap and unchanged: the weekly laundry routine (70 min), Admin on Monday evening (60 min), and the daily habits.

## Principles
- Kitchen and bathrooms keep their weekly cleaning: toilets, sinks, stove top, kitchen mop and towels all stay weekly. The bathroom and ensuite floors are still mopped and disinfected every fortnight.
- Vacuuming happens every week, alternating a full vacuum (40 min) with a new quick vacuum (15 min).
- Changes mostly stretch an interval or trim a generous effort. Nothing is removed.
- Fridge and freezer are integrated, so organising them takes less time. Bedding isn't in the list (your partner does it). The Scrub Daddy replacement every 2 months is unchanged.

## To apply
Edit each task in the app (✎ Edit task → How often / Effort). For Shred papers, use Advanced → Due weekday → Mon. Use **+ Add task** for "Quick vacuum (kitchen, hall, living area)". Then tick it once on the weekend between full vacuums, so the two alternate. The assistant can also apply all of this in one go once you say yes.

Re-run the numbers with: `node review/proposal.mjs <snapshot.json>`
